raidTAK Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026.

raidTAK is a live map for people in a short-lived room. It does not include a friends list, chat, advertising, analytics, or sale of data.

Account

You sign in with Apple. raidTAK stores the Apple-provided user identifier with the backend so you can rejoin rooms. Face ID, Touch ID, and device passcode stay on the device and are not sent to raidTAK or the backend.

Location

Precise location is used only as app functionality so people in your active room can find each other. Location is sent only while raidTAK is in the foreground and you are in a room. There is no background location. When this device leaves, you are kicked, or the room ends, this device’s location row is removed.

Display name and devices

Your display name is shown to members of the active room. The same Apple ID may join from more than one device; each device is its own dot.

Rooms

A room holds up to eight people and expires two hours after it is created, or sooner if the host ends it. If the host’s last device leaves and other people remain, host passes to someone still in the room. If the last person leaves, the room ends.

What we do not do

No tracking. No advertising. No analytics SDKs. No sale of data. Data is not used to track you across apps or websites.
